### Organization Overview

ecoPreserve is a sustainability consulting firm focused on high-performance buildings and resilient operations. They help organizations advance sustainability projects through consulting, certification support, and grant funding.

- Founded 17 years ago by Alexis Stone during the global financial crisis
- Oscar (Rollins alum, Class of 2008) joined as business manager, rose to CEO, and bought the business ~3 years ago when Alexis retired
- 50-60 client engagements per year, each lasting 6 months to 2 years
- Operates in 15 states (national only, exploring international)
- For-profit Certified B Corp (certified ~2 years ago, renewal next year, prep starting next month)
- 15 consultants plus marketing and admin staff
- Donates ~$1,500/month to a nonprofit chosen by a rotating team member
- Primary clients: local, state, and federal governments
- Too small to pursue large government contracts directly. Partners with ~30 engineering/construction firms as a sub-consultant

Oscar's background: economics degree from Rollins, master's in Australia, career in hospitality revenue management (Southwest Airlines, Expedia), pivoted to sustainability ~10 years ago with no prior experience in the field.

### Five Business Lines

1. **Green building certifications (LEED):** ~50% of revenue. Help organizations meet USGBC standards during design, construction, and operations. Currently on LEED version 5
2. **Fleet electrification:** Roadmaps for transitioning government vehicle fleets to electric (e.g., Orange County: 5,000 vehicles, 1,500 prime for electrification)
3. **Sustainability and resiliency:** Catch-all for waste management, stakeholder engagement, composting pilot programs, and related consulting
4. **Decarbonization:** Energy efficiency and electrification for facilities and fleets (e.g., Denver RTD bus system, Philadelphia International Airport sustainable aviation fuel)
5. **Grant funding:** Help organizations identify and secure funding for sustainability initiatives. Increasingly critical as federal funding landscape shifts

Notable projects: Orange County Convention Center LEED Gold expansion, Orange County fleet electrification roadmap, Denver RTD decarbonization, Philadelphia International Airport sustainable aviation fuel program.

### Current AI Usage

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| Microsoft Copilot | Primary tool. Research, drafting, refinement across Microsoft suite |
| Grammarly | Leveling communication quality across consultants |
| Google NotebookLM | Research integration, working with large data sets and multiple sources |
| BidPrime | AI-enhanced aggregator for government solicitations. Four pages of keywords, embedded AI bot for summarizing solicitations without opening documents |

**Workflow Pattern:** AI for initial research and idea dumping, human expertise for recommendations and value-add, AI again for refinement (clarity, brevity, removing redundancies).

**Key Gap: Document Formatting and Iteration.** Oscar described a concrete failure: tried to create a one-page executive summary of a greenhouse gas inventory using NotebookLM. The template came as PDF only, edits were clunky, repeated refinement made it worse. He ended up opening multiple queries, picking the best design elements from each, and manually assembling the final product.

**Solicitation Search:** BidPrime's keyword filters are too broad. Four pages of keywords still yield hundreds of irrelevant results. The AI bot helps summarize solicitations, but filtering and relevance scoring is still largely manual.

### AI Stance

No "sacred cows" like Mills Gallery has with art creation. ecoPreserve views AI as an enhancer, not a replacer. They don't delegate leadership or recommendations to AI but welcome it for research, structuring, and refinement.

Some government clients explicitly prohibit AI use on their projects (must sign a form confirming no AI was used).

Oscar was candid about a longer-term concern: at what point does AI replace consulting firms entirely? During the pandemic, ecoPreserve flipped its staffing model. They went from mostly junior consultants with 1-2 seniors to exclusively senior consultants who power their work with AI. No junior associates remain.

"A lot of the research that we used to delegate to junior associates is no longer available to them."

### Proposed Student Project Areas

1. **Green Building Certification Documentation:** Automate or streamline the LEED documentation process. Involves narratives, calculators (currently populated manually), compliance portfolios, and review packages. Green Badger has advanced automation in this space but there is room for more.
2. **Proposal Development:** Proposals are largely manual. Start from a previous proposal and adjust. Templating and automation potential. ecoPreserve churns out many proposals and feels this could be significantly streamlined.
3. **Solicitation Search Optimization:** Improve keyword filtering and relevance scoring in the BidPrime workflow. Reduce visual noise from irrelevant results. Help the team get from thousands of weekly solicitations to the handful that actually matter.

### Key Quotes
