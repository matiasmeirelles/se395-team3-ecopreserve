# ecoPreserve: Proposed Student Project Areas

## 1. Green Building Certification Documentation

Automate or streamline the LEED documentation process. Currently involves:
- Narratives describing the sustainability strategy
- Calculators that are populated manually
- Compliance portfolios
- Review packages uploaded to USGBC

Green Badger has advanced automation in this space but there is room for more.

## 2. Proposal Development

Proposals are largely manual. They start from a previous proposal and adjust each time. ecoPreserve churns out many proposals and feels this could be significantly streamlined through templating and automation.

## 3. Solicitation Search Optimization

Improve keyword filtering and relevance scoring in the BidPrime workflow. The goal is to reduce visual noise from hundreds of irrelevant results and help the team get to the handful of solicitations that actually matter each week.

## Open to Other Ideas

These are starting points. Oscar also mentioned interest in:
- Newsletter automation (10,000 sustainability leaders, content currently created manually)
- Impact metrics storytelling (using AI to tell a more articulate story of their project impacts)

All alternative project scopes must be approved by the partner and the instructor.
