# ecoPreserve: Key Quotes from Oscar

On mission-driven for-profit work:
> "Part of our mission is to prove that you can actually do this and create a great life for yourselves by living in harmony with your values."

On AI in consulting:
> "AI is an enhancer, not a replacer. We don't delegate the leadership and the ability to create valuable recommendations for our clients based on real experience."

On the future of consulting:
> "I question at what point AI would be capable of replacing us. And in the consulting industry, it is a valuable concern. As things get easier, more accessible, more sophisticated, where you plug in yourself needs to elevate."

On not convincing clients:
> "We don't convince anyone to do this. We empower leaders who want to do it and who don't have the expertise or the team or the funding. We're not in the business of changing hearts."

On the sustainability community:
> "One of the best things about the sustainability space are the human beings in that space. My partners, my clients, even my competitors are amazing human beings."

On the staffing shift:
> "A lot of the research that we used to delegate to junior associates is no longer available to them."
